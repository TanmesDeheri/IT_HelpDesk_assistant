## How do I reset my password?
1. Go to portal.artscape.internal/reset.
2. Enter your work email.
3. Follow the link sent to your email to set a new password.
4. If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, contact it-support@artscape.com.
## How do I request VPN access?
1. Log in to the IT Service Portal.
2. Click "New Request" → "VPN Access."
3. Your manager will receive an approval request.
4. Once approved, you'll receive VPN credentials within 1 business day.
## How do I report a lost or stolen device?
1. Immediately email it-support@artscape.com with subject line "URGENT: Lost/Stolen
Device."
2. Include device serial number (found on the bottom of the laptop) if known.
3. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device within 1 hour of report.
## How do I request new software?
1. First check the Software Catalog on the IT Portal — most common tools are pre-approved.
2. If not listed, submit a "Software Request" ticket with business justification.
3. Approval typically takes 2-3 business days.
## Who do I contact for hardware issues (laptop not turning on, broken screen, etc.)?
- Email it-support@artscape.com with a description of the issue and your asset tag number.

- For urgent hardware failures, walk-in support is available at the IT desk, 3rd floor, 10 AM–4 PM on weekdays.
