## Password Policy
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters, including one number and one special
character.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Passwords cannot be reused for the last 5
changes.
- Account lockout occurs after 5 failed login attempts, requiring IT to unlock.

## Device Policy
- Company laptops are provided to full-time employees; contractors must use personal
devices with VPN access.
- All company devices must have antivirus and disk encryption enabled before first use.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to it-support@artscape.com within 24 hours.
## Software Installation Policy
- Employees may install approved software from the internal Software Catalog without
approval.
- Any software outside the catalog requires manager + IT approval before installation.
- Personal/non-work software installation on company devices is not permitted.
## VPN & Remote Access Policy
- VPN access is mandatory when connecting to internal systems from outside the office
network.
- VPN credentials are separate from email login and must be requested via the IT portal.
- VPN sessions automatically disconnect after 8 hours of inactivity.
## Data Backup Policy
- Company laptops back up automatically to OneDrive every 24 hours when connected to Wi-
Fi.
- Employees are responsible for ensuring OneDrive sync is active; IT does not maintain
separate manual backups.
