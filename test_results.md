## How do I reset my password?
To reset your password, follow these steps:

1. Go to portal.artscape.internal/reset.


2. Enter your work email.


3. Follow the link sent to your email to set a new password.


4. If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, contact it-support@artscape.com.



Source: **it_faq_v1.0.md - How do I reset my password?**

## My laptop screen is cracked, what do I do?
For hardware issues like a broken laptop screen, you should report the problem using the following steps:

1. Email it-support@artscape.com with a description of the issue and your asset tag number.


2. For urgent hardware failures, walk-in support is available at the IT desk on the 3rd floor, open from 10 AM to 4 PM on weekdays.



Source: **it_faq_v1.0.md - Who do I contact for hardware issues (laptop not turning on, broken screen, etc.)?**
